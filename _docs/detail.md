My recommendation

If your goal is to impress this client, don't stop at three screenshots. Build a cohesive demo with 6–10 polished screens using a consistent design system. Then submit the three strongest screenshots (such as Create Exam, Question Builder, and Candidate Registration) in your proposal. If the client asks for more, you'll already have an entire enterprise-quality Angular application ready to showcase.
